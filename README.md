# HERMETICA Sports

Build the Ultimate Sports OS app incorporating both the Ratchaburi FC home theme and the Minnesota Twin Cities theme and tracker file:

1. Dual-Home Architecture (Ratchaburi FC + Minnesota Hub):
- Quick hub toggle in the header between Ratchaburi FC (Dragon Solar Park, orange theme #FF6600, ICT time Asia/Bangkok, live weather) and Minnesota Twin Cities Hub (Vikings purple & gold #4F2683/#FFC62F, dual CT/ICT time).
- Also support viewing all teams together.

2. Full incorporation of all 7 Minnesota teams from the uploaded file:
- Minnesota Vikings (NFL): Seal VIK, #4F2683 / #FFC62F, 2-0 record, Flores D and Wentz/Jones highlights, next game Sun Sep 28 12:00pm CT • Mon 12:00am ICT @ Steelers (Dublin - Croke Park)
- Minnesota Timberwolves (NBA): Seal WOLVES, #0C2340 / #78BE20, 49-33 record, Ant + Gobert core, training camp & preseason notes, Target Center
- Minnesota Twins (MLB): Seal TC, #002B5C / #D31145, 73-83 record, Target Field schedule, Roden HR, Kremer 6IP recaps
- Minnesota Wild (NHL): Seal WILD, #154734 / #A6192E, 46-24-12 record, Kaprizov/Boldy lines, Xcel Energy Center schedule vs Blackhawks
- Minnesota Lynx (WNBA): Seal LYNX, #00A9E0 / #236192, 32-10 record, Collier & Miles records, 1 win from #1 seed, Target Center playoffs
- Minnesota Frost (PWHL): Seal FROST, #5B2C83 / #FFFFFF, 16-9-5 • 50 PTS, back-to-back Walter Cup Champions, Knight/Heise/Coyne
- Minnesota United FC (MLS): Seal MNUFC, #231F20 / #8CB5E5, 7-12-8 record, Allianz Field schedule, Ruiz FK recap, fan mood notes

3. Master Plan Features:
- Universal multi-sport coverage across NFL, NBA, MLB, Premier League, La Liga, Thai League 1, F1, WNBA, NHL, PWHL, MLS
- Five core sections: LIVE, MY TEAMS, EXPLORE, LEAGUES, FANTASY
- Live score ticker with odds and real-time game simulation
- Dual Time Display: Show both US Central Time (CT) and Thailand Time (ICT) side by side for all Minnesota games
- Smart search with autocomplete (English & Thai) and 1-tap/long-press add to favorites
- "Add All Minnesota Teams" 1-tap quick action in Explore / My Teams
- SofaScore-style player ratings, attack momentum bars, possession, shots, and fan sentiment indicators (e.g. 88% Loved, 38% Frustrated)
- Prediction mini-game with leaderboard and LINE sharing
- Thai/English (TH/EN) bilingual toggle
- Calendar export (.ics / Google Calendar) and shareable match cards
- Responsive layout with bottom safe-area nav on mobile and top tabs on desktop
